# Cadence

An online course platform where Students buy Courses and work through their lessons.

## Language

### People

**Student**:
A person who buys and takes Courses.
_Avoid_: Learner

### Buying a Course

**Purchase**:
A Student paying for a Course.

**Team Seat**:
A place on a Course bought by a team and claimed by a Student with a coupon. A claimed Team Seat cannot be refunded.
_Avoid_: Seat licence

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A Student's request for their money back on a Purchase, with their reason. It is Pending until someone Approves or Declines it.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, cancel my purchase, Reversal, Chargeback

**Pending**:
The state of a Refund Request that no one has decided on yet.
_Avoid_: Requested, Open

**Approved**:
The outcome of a Refund Request that is granted, leading to a Refund.
_Avoid_: Accepted

**Declined**:
The outcome of a Refund Request that is turned down. No money is given back.
_Avoid_: Rejected, Denied

**Refund**:
The money actually given back to a Student after their Refund Request is Approved.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days after a Purchase in which a Student can raise a Refund Request.
